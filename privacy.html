Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AVIX ("AVIX," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use AVIX at avix.study and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: - Account information — your name and email address, created when you sign up. Your password is handled and stored by our authentication provider (Supabase); we do not store your raw password. - Content you submit — your chat messages and questions, oral-exam practice answers, study preferences and saved topics, and aircraft (N-numbers) you look up and save. - Payment information — when you subscribe, your payment is processed by Stripe. We receive limited billing details (such as subscription status and the last four digits / card brand from Stripe), but we do not receive or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically: - Technical and usage data — such as IP address, browser type, and request logs, used for security, rate limiting, and to operate the Service. - Local storage — we store your login token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We use this for authentication, not for advertising.

Information from third parties: - FAA registry data — when you look up an aircraft, we retrieve publicly available registration details (such as make/model, year, and the registered owner's name, city, and state) from the FAA aircraft registry and may store them with your saved aircraft.

2. How we use information

We use information to: - provide, maintain, and secure the Service and your account; - generate AI responses, study materials, and practice evaluations, and personalize features such as your homepage greeting and aircraft-tailored study; - process subscriptions and payments (via Stripe); - prevent fraud and abuse and enforce our Terms; and - communicate with you about your account or the Service.

3. AI processing of your inputs

To generate responses and study materials, the content you submit (e.g., your questions and answers) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, for processing. Do not submit sensitive personal information you would not want processed this way. Anthropic processes this data under its own terms; we do not use your content to train third-party models, and we do not sell your data.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only: - With service providers ("sub-processors") who help us run the Service, including Anthropic (AI), Stripe (payments), Supabase (authentication and database), and our hosting provider (Render), each under their own privacy and security obligations; - For legal reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our rights, users, or the public; and - In a business transfer — e.g., a merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. We also run automated cleanup that, by default: - deletes inactive chat sessions after about 30 days of inactivity; - archives and then removes oral-exam results after about 90 days; and - removes expired free-trial records after about 60 days.

You can delete your saved aircraft and chats within the app, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting avix.flight@gmail.com. Some records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA (including the right to know and delete, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising rights); EU/UK residents may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR. To exercise any right, contact avix.flight@gmail.com; we will respond as required by applicable law. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), authenticated access controls, and database row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults (18+) preparing for an FAA certificate and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

We operate the Service from the United States, and your information will be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our service providers operate. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., you consent to this transfer and processing.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date and, for material changes, provide additional notice where required.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: avix.flight@gmail.com · AVIX (mailing address available on request).
