# AVIX — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AVIX ("AVIX," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use AVIX at avix.study and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you provide:**
- **Account information** — your name and email address, created when you sign up. Your password is handled and stored by our authentication provider (Supabase); we do not store your raw password.
- **Content you submit** — your chat messages and questions, oral-exam practice answers, study preferences and saved topics, and aircraft (N-numbers) you look up and save.
- **Payment information** — when you subscribe, your payment is processed by Stripe. We receive limited billing details (such as subscription status and the last four digits / card brand from Stripe), but **we do not receive or store your full card number.**

**Information collected automatically:**
- **Technical and usage data** — such as IP address, browser type, and request logs, used for security, rate limiting, and to operate the Service.
- **Local storage** — we store your login token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We use this for authentication, not for advertising.

**Information from third parties:**
- **FAA registry data** — when you look up an aircraft, we retrieve publicly available registration details (such as make/model, year, and the registered owner's name, city, and state) from the FAA aircraft registry and may store them with your saved aircraft.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:
- provide, maintain, and secure the Service and your account;
- generate AI responses, study materials, and practice evaluations, and personalize features such as your homepage greeting and aircraft-tailored study;
- process subscriptions and payments (via Stripe);
- prevent fraud and abuse and enforce our Terms; and
- communicate with you about your account or the Service.

## 3. AI processing of your inputs
